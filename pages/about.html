OUR STORY

Designing Spaces That Reflect Your Soul

Founded by Anuradha Chadha, Design Harmony stands at the intersection of aesthetic sophistication, ergonomic practical utility, and ancient spatial science. With over 20 years of hands-on experience in interior design, project management, Vastu Shastra, and Date of Birth numerology, Anuradha brings a holistic perspective to every project.

We believe that a space is not merely four walls filled with furniture. It is a living environment that shapes your mindset, energy levels, personal well-being, and professional productivity.

Whether crafting a serene private residence, an inspiring corporate headquarters, or an engaging retail store, our commitment is to listen deeply, plan meticulously, and execute flawlessly.
