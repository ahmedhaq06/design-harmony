Importance of Directions in Vastu

Directions are one of the most important foundations of Vastu Shastra. The eight main directions—North, North-East, East, South-East, South, South-West, West, and North-West—are associated with different elements and aspects of life.

A proper Vastu analysis studies how these directions interact with your main entrance, kitchen, bedrooms, toilets, Mandir, work areas, and furniture placement. At Design Harmony, we carefully evaluate these factors to identify imbalances and recommend practical solutions that complement both Vastu and Interior Design.
