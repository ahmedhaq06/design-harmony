Practical Vastu for Real Spaces

Vastu Shastra is the traditional Indian science of architecture that studies directional energy flows and environmental harmony. At Design Harmony, Anuradha Chadha provides scientific, practical Vastu consultation that avoids unnecessary structural demolition wherever possible.

Whether evaluating a plot before purchase, auditing an existing apartment, or arranging executive seating in a corporate suite, our remedies focus on color balancing, placement, lighting, and elemental alignments.
